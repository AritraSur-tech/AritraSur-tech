Tips for Beginners

  • Weigh yourself at the same time each day (morning is best)
  • Don't worry about daily fluctuations - focus on weekly trends
  • Aim for 0.5-1 kg per week for healthy weight loss
  • Stay hydrated and get enough sleep for accurate readings
